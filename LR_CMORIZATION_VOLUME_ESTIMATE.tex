\sect{Result}
LR writes everything HR writes, plus the rules deactivated for HR, plus REcoM. That is \textbf{about 600 to 710
files per model year}, against 502 for HR, and \textbf{about 110\,GB per year} if daily 3D biogeochemistry is
left out, or \textbf{about 160\,GB} with it. The grids are much smaller than HR, so the larger file count
comes to between a quarter and a third of HR's 470\,GB.

\sect{Everything HR writes, scaled to LR}
HR's cmorized volume was measured per grid on one full model year and divided by the ratio of cells, for 3D ocean
fields also by the ratio of vertical levels. Coarse grids compress slightly worse per value and the fixed overhead
per file does not shrink, so this part lies between 40 and 55\,GB.

\sect{How the added parts were estimated}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Reactivated rules.} 44 rules are commented out for HR; 11 of them are produced by another active rule
      already, which leaves 33 new files. Sizes of the sub-daily atmosphere sets come from HR's measured compressed
      bytes per stored value (about 1\,byte on model levels, 1.7--2.7\,bytes on pressure levels), applied to the LR grid.
      The model-level range reflects 91 or 137 vertical levels, which was not confirmed for LR.
\item \textbf{REcoM, files.} The test run \code{RECOM\_concdriven\_04} writes 69 biogeochemistry streams. The CMIP7 data
      request has 178 ocean biogeochemistry entries, 44 of them Southern Ocean subsets, and several entries derive
      from the same stream (surface values, integrals, sums over plankton groups). The real count is likely near 178.
\item \textbf{REcoM, volume.} The 69 streams are 11\,GB per year of raw output, all monthly. Cmorizing keeps 82--94\,\%
      of monthly 3D ocean fields (measured on HR \code{temp} and \code{salt}), about 10\,GB, plus a few GB for surface
      and regional entries. The seven daily 3D fields are about 7.6\,GB each and need daily REcoM output.
\end{itemize}

\sect{Not covered}
Compute time (HR's 33 node-hours per year is I/O-bound and does not scale reliably) and the length of each LR
experiment; all numbers are per model year.
